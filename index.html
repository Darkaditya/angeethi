Our Story

A hearth, not just a kitchen

Angeethi began as a single charcoal hearth in a family courtyard — the word itself means the warm iron pot that keeps a home fed through winter. Three generations later, that same fire still anchors every dish that leaves our kitchen.

Every recipe is slow, deliberate, and built on spice blends ground fresh each morning. Nothing here is rushed — not the marinade, not the service, not the evening.
